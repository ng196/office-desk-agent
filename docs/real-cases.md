### Key findings for the builder
1. Font names lie or are missing. 2 of 6 legacy PDFs have no Kruti/Chanakya name in pdffonts. Detection must use text statistics too, not names alone.
2. Kruti Dev and Chanakya encode the same word differently (`çkf/kdkj` vs `izkf/dkj`). Per-span profile needed.
3. "Unicode" Hindi PDFs from government (CSMOP Hindi, Rajbhasha circular) still extract wrong (`सविि` for सचिव). Need a quality check plus OCR fallback even when fonts look fine.
4. Search-engine summaries got rule numbers wrong: one claimed CCS Pension Rules 2021 "Rule 68" gives interest; the PDF shows interest is **Rule 65** (Rule 68 is recovery of accommodation dues). Same risk for the local LLM, so the exact-quote checker matters.

Skipped / unchecked: cic.gov.in's own decision search (not tried), CPGRAMS annual reports, Hindi RTI Act PDF, DoPT leave/pension OMs beyond the rules. Sample reuse rights rest on Copyright Act s.52(1)(q), not legal advice.
